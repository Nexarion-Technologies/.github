# Community conduct

Nexarion public repositories are technical workspaces. Participate professionally, keep discussion relevant to the project, and treat contributors with basic respect.

Expected behavior includes:

- discussing ideas and defects without attacking people;
- providing reproducible technical evidence where practical;
- respecting privacy, confidentiality, licenses, and intellectual-property boundaries;
- avoiding harassment, discrimination, threats, spam, impersonation, and deliberate disruption;
- reporting security issues privately rather than using them for public spectacle.

Maintainers may edit, hide, or remove content and may restrict participation when conduct interferes with a safe and useful technical environment.

Report conduct concerns privately to `info@nexarion.tech`. Include only the information necessary to investigate the issue.
